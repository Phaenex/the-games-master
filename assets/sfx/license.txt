THE NINTH BELL - FINAL CURATED CUES (2026-07-20)
- clock_chime.ogg  Derived from "G25-02-Grandfather Clock Chime.wav" by craigsmith.
                 License: CC0 1.0. Source Freesound ID 438313. One physical strike is isolated
                 from 2.20-3.62s, then given a project-authored room decay. It contains no second
                 clock strike.
                 https://freesound.org/people/craigsmith/sounds/438313/
- heartbeat.ogg  Derived from "Heartbeat" by JonasTisell, an actual resting heartbeat recorded
                 from Andy Jack. License: CC0 1.0. Source Freesound ID 670465. Seven complete beat
                 cycles are boundary-trimmed into a click-safe loop.
                 https://freesound.org/people/JonasTisell/sounds/670465/
- whisper_bed.ogg  Derived from "Whisper.wav" by PlumForestPodcast, described by its author as a
                 quiet whisper with no meaning. License: CC0 1.0. Source Freesound ID 517868.
                 Curated as a non-looping nine-second crossing cue with restrained stereo motion.
                 https://freesound.org/people/PlumForestPodcast/sounds/517868/
- ear_whine.ogg  Project-authored tinnitus cue. Deterministic periodic synthesis by this project,
                 2026-07-20. It intentionally uses narrow high tones because it is an internal
                 symptom, not environmental ambience. Eight-second seam-safe stereo loop. CC0.

The reproducible, SHA-256-pinned curation pipeline is scripts/gen-bell-audio.py. The shipping OGG
files are transformed in-game assets; the three Freesound sources are CC0 and require no credit,
but remain credited here for durable provenance.
