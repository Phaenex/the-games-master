# The Games Master — Canonical Narrative & World Bible

## 1. Setting & Atmosphere
- **Location**: Wend Hill Estate — a remote Victorian/Edwardian manor estate built on the foundations of an ancient coaching inn.
- **Era / Technology**: 1920s period authenticity. Handheld illumination uses brass carbide inspection lamps, oil/tallow candle tapers, and paraffin/gas flickering post lamps. No modern electric flashlights or plastic materials.
- **Atmosphere**: Claustrophobic gothic horror, dense pine/fir canopies, slatted moonlight shafts, weathered stone Celtic crosses, stopped clocks (frozen at 8:59), rotting timber, and sulfur matches.

## 2. Core Narrative & Antagonist
- **The Protagonist**: An estranged debtor summoned to Wend Hill to clear generational estate debts by playing the parlor games of "The Games Master."
- **The Host (Aldric Voss / The Games Master)**: A supernatural host bound to the estate. He cheats systematically in every game through mechanical devices, palmed cards, and rigged dice, yet secretly tests whether the player has the acuity to catch his tells.
- **The Nine Debtors**: Past victims whose oil portraits hang in the Grand Entry Hall (Percival, Marr, etc.). They failed the games and were absorbed into the estate's fabric.

## 3. The 3 Mirror Shards
- **Shard 1 (Entry Hall)**: Hidden behind Percival's oil portrait frame (unvarnished wood backing & loosened canvas).
- **Shard 2 (The Court Room)**: Unlocked beneath the hollow base of the witness stand upon cracking the judicial gavel seal with contradictory evidence.
- **Shard 3 (The Hidden Study)**: Found inside the private safe within the subterranean brick labyrinth after following the floor compass markings.

## 4. The 4 Branching Endings
1. **Ending 1: The Games Master's Successor (Complicit Victory)**: Win the games without all 3 shards. Accept the host's ring and become the next Games Master.
2. **Ending 2: The Debtor's Ruin (Default / Defeat)**: Fail the games or get caught cheating 3 times. Absorbed as the 10th portrait in the Grand Hall.
3. **Ending 3: The True Escape (Canon Whistleblower)**: 3 Mirror Shards + 8+ Caught Tells + Broken Gavel. Escape through the chapel crypt into the dawn fog.
4. **Ending 4: The Turnaround (Cold Open Secret)**: Refuse the invitation at the car and drive away before the iron gate locks.

## 5. Narrative Locks & Threshold Refusal
- The manor front door **never opens on foot**. When the player reaches the porch, the Ninth Bell toll rings across the estate, transitioning the player directly into the Wake Room.
