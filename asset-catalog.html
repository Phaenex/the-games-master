✓ Resolved this session

Both manually downloaded, compared side-by-side, and decided:

Mansion exterior — chose "Haunted Victorian House" by gravyart over "HAUNTED MANSION" by n-malmberg. Both were CC-BY and similarly licensed, but a direct visual comparison settled it: n-malmberg's model rendered dark and murky with visible geometry gaps in the porch area, while gravyart's has real clapboard siding, window/shutter detail, a wraparound porch and staircase, and a widow's-walk tower — it actually reads as a Victorian mansion. The extra triangle count (216.9k vs 68.6k) earns its keep. Fence/gate — swapped the generic Quaternius "Metal Fence" (a flat industrial guardrail, wrong style entirely) for plaggy's ornate wrought-iron picket fence with spear-tip finials — genuinely period-appropriate, not just technically available.

Ground textures — already covered, no action needed

Brick, grass, stone, and wood tileable textures (CC0, Poly Haven) already exist in assets/tex/ from earlier sourcing this project — these cover the walkway/grounds surface. Nothing new needed here.
