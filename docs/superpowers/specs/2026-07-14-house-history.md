## Core rule

The **player** lives in ~2018–2026. The **house face** the player walks through is stuck in a late Victorian / Edwardian “invitation house” dress. Outside time keeps moving. Inside dress does not.

**Site name (canon, added 2026-07-16):** the property is “the house on Wend Hill.” The name first
appears on the Prologue invitation and was canonized from there — every later reference (host
speech, documents, endings) uses “Wend Hill,” never a street address, never a family name for the
house itself. “Wend” = an old word for a turn in a road; the drive that turns you toward the house
is the name. Locals would say “the Wend Hill house.”

The house is not a museum tour. Layers show as scars, wrong nails, one object that doesn’t belong to the current furniture set, and host speech that names a room by an older word. Never a plaque. Never a timeline UI.

---

## Why this history (not a saloon)

The live exterior is a tall Victorian pile with porch, lamps, iron fence, cemetery wing, ruined garden. That silhouette is an English-or-New-England **coaching corridor / country estate** building, not a prairie main-street saloon.

What *does* fit — same functions Nick cared about (drink, lodgers, gambling, performance, rooms for hire):

| Urge | Fit on this building |
|------|----------------------|
| People drank here | Public **taproom** (coaching inn) |
| People slept upstairs | Overnight rooms → later club chambers |
| People played for money | Cards upstairs → private club → invitation games |
| Stage / raised end | Reading / music platform in the old public room (now Court floor join) |
| Hitching | Coach horses / posts by the porch line |

So the oldest *readable* public life is a **coaching inn**, not a Western saloon. Same archaeology. Correct architecture.

## Timeline of the site (canon)

Dates stay soft on purpose. Exact years belong to optional host slips, not HUD.

### Layer 5 — Underside (`L5_underside`)

Cellars, drains, older brick bond, corridors that match no guest floorplan. Labyrinth. Prefers not to be named. Host almost never references this; the building does.

### Layer 4 — Coaching inn (`L4_coaching_inn`)

Public house on a road that mattered once. Taproom on the ground floor. Rooms for travellers above. Boot rail at bar height. Pewter rings. Coach-lamp / hitching scars at the porch. A raised strip at one end of the big room for a fiddler or a reading (the Court still sits on that join).

This is the oldest *guest-visible* memory of the public spaces. Host at high Corruption may say “bar,” “tap,” “public room,” “the old road,” or “we kept whiskey before we kept ledgers.”

### Layer 3 — Private club (`L3_club`)

Road quieted. Someone turned lodgers into **members**. Numbered brass room markers. Lobby desk footprint where guests signed in. Card tables without the invitation ceremony. Shut the Box’s hall still wears a dust-sheet over a framed corner that was once a house rule / evening card notice, not a saloon showbill.

Host slips: “member,” “the desk,” “your room,” “the club books.”

### Layer 2 — Private manor (`L2_manor`)

A short, failed attempt to make it a normal family house. Arches filled. Numbers painted over. Servant-bell stubs. Wallpaper over club paper. Aldric’s sealed chamber sits in this transitional scar zone — a guest room the house forgot after he took the chair.

Host almost never romanticizes this era. It was thin and brief.

### Layer 1 — Invitation games house (`L1_gameshouse`)

The dress the player meets. Parlor, Entry Hall with nine portraits, ledger, oil lamps, Court as Assize, Shut the Box as the quieter hall. Furniture MetalMan / Victorian. **Frozen.** Guests arrive across centuries of outside time and find the same century of wallpaper.

Aldric’s long tenure keeps this dress. Hosts before him may have already locked it; the player does not need to know which host hung which portrait. The ledger’s selectivity (nine names) is story-bible canon — most visitors leave no durable mark.

### Layer 0 — Modern guest (`L0_modern`)

Only what rides in with tonight’s visitor: modern car, mud from outside shoes, contemporary paper stock on the invitation, optional phone that never works here. The house does not absorb these into L1. They remain wrong on purpose.

## Host slip vocabulary (subtle)

Use **sparingly**. Prefer one wrong word, not a paragraph.

| Prefer | Avoid |
|--------|-------|
| tap / the bar / public room | saloon, sheriff, cowboy, prairie |
| the desk / member / overnight | hotel lobby modern |
| back room | “conference room,” Wi-Fi jokes |
| the old road | highway, interstate |
| books / ledger (double meaning ok) | spreadsheet |

Example tone (not final VO):

- “Take the **bar** — forgive me. The rail. Present your case.”
- “We kept **whiskey** in this footing before we kept debts.”
- “Someone else’s **number** is still under that paper. Ignore it.”
