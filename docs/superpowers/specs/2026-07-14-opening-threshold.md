## Decision (locked)

**No second mansion purchase.** Gravyart shell + Env dress + Door_Double leaves stay. The opening does **not** depend on selling a perfect hinge-and-walk-in entrance.

**Canon beat name:** Threshold Refusal.

---

## What happens (player-facing)

1. Walk the drive. Gate locks behind. Estate reads as a real approach.
2. Climb the porch. The **front doors stay closed.** Warm light seeps at a seam, then stills.
3. You are **not admitted as a caller.** Something takes you from the porch dark (collar / blow / the house’s side of the bargain) — **not** from swinging the doors open.
4. Blackout.
5. Wake on Entry Hall marble (`Cold. The marble had my cheek.`). The inside is the first room you get. You never earned the civil doorway.

This is already how the code works (`closed-door trap` in Prologue). Narration and intent now match.
