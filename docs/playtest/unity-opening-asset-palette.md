# Unity Opening Asset Palette

This is the Wend Hill opening whitelist. Downloaded bundle content is a library, not a mandate to
place everything. Every approved prefab below has one scene role; unlisted hero architecture and
occult/fantasy props remain out of the opening until deliberately reviewed.

| Zone | Approved owned assets | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival | `RealisticCar03_HD_Exterior_LOD0`, `graveyard_gate` | Present-day guest arrival and the point of no return |
| Woodland | filtered dead trees, `SM_DeadWillow`, `SM_DeadBush_01/02`, small rocks, twig piles, wood logs | Bare silhouette layers and low dead groundcover |
| Cemetery | `SM_StoneWall_01`, `SM_GraveCross_01/02/03`, dead bushes, small rocks, twig piles | Low enclosure, family plots, accumulated scruff |
| Garden | `Sm_Fence_01/02`, dead bushes, twig piles, wood logs, small rocks, four darkened `SM_Pumpkin` instances | Neglected bed rows, ordinary leftover produce, boundary, shed-side work debris |
| Coach yard | `SM_Cart`, `SM_FeedingTrough`, `SM_Barrel_01`, `SM_Crate_01/02`, `SM_HayStack`, `SM_WoodenWheel_01`, `SM_Bucket`, wood logs, `SM_Lantern` | Believable loading/feed/work clusters with a clear turning area |
| Mansion | existing gravyart Haunted Victorian House, owned lantern sources | Single sealed house, restrained occupied-window read |

## Rejected for this phase

- Any second mansion, hut, village shell, or full downloaded demo scene.
- Witch Village totems, ritual props, fantasy signage, and green/glowing vegetation.
- Witch Village architecture remains excluded; the ordinary HDRP pumpkin prop is the sole reviewed
  exception and is used in two small kitchen-garden clusters, not as occult/Halloween dressing.
- Built-in-pipeline prefab twins when an HDRP variant exists.
- `SM_DriedLeaves*` in height-normalized scatter: their nearly flat bounds expand into giant planes.
- Generic Horror Elements space, underwater, tonal-rumble, and synthetic-drone clips as exterior beds.
- Decorative colliders on backdrop terrain, woodland, or flank groundcover.

The modern SUV stays because the guest is contemporary. Its final art-direction verdict remains a
Nick taste gate; it is not replaced or hidden during the agent pass.
